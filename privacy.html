This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, stored and protected when you visit the RYXO website, contact us, participate in a survey, or join the RYXO waitlist or newsletter.

Depending on how you use this website, we may process the following information:

  • technical information generated when accessing the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, date and time of access and requested pages.

Some surveys are designed not to request directly identifying information such as your name or email address. Technical information may nevertheless be processed by the services used to provide the form or website.

4. Why we process your data

We process personal data only where necessary for purposes connected with the RYXO project.

These purposes may include:

  • managing the RYXO waitlist;
  • sending launch updates and newsletters where you have subscribed;
  • conducting market research and analysing survey responses;
  • responding to questions or messages;
  • improving the website and the RYXO project;
  • maintaining the security and technical operation of the website;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.
