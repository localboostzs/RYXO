THE DATA

BACKED BY RESEARCH

Energy drinks are widely consumed — but caffeine isn’t wanted in every situation. Research shows that caffeine can affect sleep, while taste remains an important reason why people choose energy drinks. That is the gap RYXO is exploring.

01

Caffeine & Sleep

The European Food Safety Authority states that even 100 mg of caffeine may affect sleep duration and sleep patterns in some adults, particularly when consumed close to bedtime.

View EFSA research →
02

Energy Drinks Are Mainstream

A European survey by EFSA found that 68% of surveyed adolescents aged 10–18 had consumed energy drinks, showing how established the category has become among younger consumers.

View EFSA study →
03

Taste Matters

Research on energy drink consumers found taste to be an important reason for consumption. Among participants with a preferred brand, 86% reported choosing that brand because of its taste.
