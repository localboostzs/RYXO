THE IDEA

SO, WHAT IS RYXO?

RYXO is a new caffeine-free drink brand built around one simple idea: you shouldn't need caffeine just to enjoy the Energy experience.

We're currently creating the first RYXO drink. It is being designed to be intense, modern and best served cold from the can — for daytime, late nights and every moment in between.

  • 0 mg caffeine
  • Intense, modern taste
  • Made for the can
  • A lifestyle drink, not a compromise
