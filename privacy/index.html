2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, stored and protected when you visit the RYXO website, contact us, participate in a survey, or join the RYXO waitlist or newsletter.

We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Where applicable, we also take into account the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Personal data we may collect

Depending on how you use this website, we may process the following information:

  • your name, if you choose to provide it;
  • your email address;
  • information you submit through surveys, forms or waitlists;
  • messages and information you send when contacting us;
  • technical information generated when accessing the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, date and time of access and requested pages.

Some surveys are designed not to request directly identifying information such as your name or email address. Technical information may nevertheless be processed by the services used to provide the form or website.

4. Why we process your data

We process personal data only where necessary for purposes connected with the RYXO project.

These purposes may include:

  • managing the RYXO waitlist;
  • sending launch updates and newsletters where you have subscribed;
  • conducting market research and analysing survey responses;
  • responding to questions or messages;
  • measuring the reach of the website in aggregated form;
  • improving the website and the RYXO project;
  • maintaining the security and technical operation of the website;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Waitlist and newsletter

If you voluntarily join the RYXO waitlist or newsletter, we may use the information you provide to send you information about RYXO, including product development, launches, flavours, availability, early access and other relevant updates.

You may withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time.

If an unsubscribe link is included in an email, you can use that link. You may also contact us at:

contact@businessjourney26.com

After unsubscribing, your email address may be retained only where necessary to document your opt-out or comply with legal obligations.

6. Waitlist form provided through Formspree

The waitlist form on this website is provided through Formspree, a form service operated by Formspree, Inc. in the United States.

When you submit the form, your email address and technical data required for the submission (such as IP address and browser information) are transmitted to Formspree, stored there and forwarded to us. Formspree also uses this data to protect the form against spam and misuse.

Formspree processes this data on our behalf. More information is available in the Formspree Privacy Policy.

7. Forms and surveys provided through Tally

We currently use Tally to provide certain forms and surveys on or in connection with this website.

The survey on this website is only loaded from Tally's servers once you open it. From that moment, Tally may process technical data such as your IP address and browser information.

When you submit information through a Tally form, the information is processed through Tally's infrastructure on our behalf.

According to Tally, the form creator acts as the controller of submitted form data and Tally acts as the processor. Tally states that form data is encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in Europe. Tally may use additional subprocessors in connection with its services. Tally Forms

Information submitted through Tally may include, depending on the specific form:

  • email address;
  • name;
  • survey answers;
  • demographic information voluntarily provided;
  • other information entered into a form.

We only use this information for the purpose stated in or around the relevant form.

8. Hosting, domain and website operation

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. The domain ryxoenergy.com is registered and managed through Cloudflare, Inc. in the United States.

When you access this website, technical data is processed automatically by these providers to deliver the website. This may include information such as your IP address, browser, operating system, device type, requested page and the date and time of access. This data may be stored temporarily in server logs.

Such processing is necessary to provide the website, maintain security, detect misuse and ensure reliable operation. More information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

9. Fonts and scripts provided through jsDelivr

To display fonts and the 3D animations, this website loads font files (Montserrat and Inter) and program libraries (three.js) from the content delivery network jsDelivr.

When these files are loaded, your browser connects to jsDelivr's servers. In doing so, your IP address and technical browser information are transmitted to jsDelivr and its infrastructure providers. This is necessary to display the website as intended. The fonts are not loaded from Google.

More information is available in the jsDelivr Privacy Policy.

10. Website analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., to understand how many people visit the website and which pages are viewed.

According to Cloudflare, this service does not use cookies, does not store data in your browser and does not use fingerprinting to track individual visitors across websites. It records information such as the page viewed, referrer, browser type, device type, country and loading times. We only see aggregated statistics.

We use this service based on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the website. More information is available in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

The RYXO website itself does not set cookies for analytics or advertising. Cloudflare Web Analytics works without cookies.

Embedded third-party services, in particular the Tally survey once opened, may use technically necessary cookies or similar technologies required for functionality, security or form operation.

If we introduce non-essential tracking or advertising technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy and, where required, the website's consent mechanisms will be updated accordingly.
